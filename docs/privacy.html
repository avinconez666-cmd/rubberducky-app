Privacy policy

10/4 Rubberducky version 1.0. Last updated 22 October 2026.

This app is a driver radio that runs on your phone. It does not create an account, and version 1.0 does not upload your location, voice, messages, or hazard pins to us.

Location

If you allow it, the app reads your location while the app is open. It uses that to show your pin on the map, measure speed for Safety Mode, estimate distance to a place you searched, and save a hazard pin. Location is not collected in the background and is not sold or shared with other drivers in this version.

Microphone

If you allow it, holding the push-to-talk button records a short clip stored on your phone so you can play it back. Clips are not uploaded.

What stays on the phone

Your handle, vehicle type, channels, convoy names, hazard notes, and message log are stored in the app’s local storage on the device. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Map tiles and place search

The map loads tiles from OpenStreetMap. Place search uses Nominatim (also OpenStreetMap). Those services see the tile or search request from your device, the same way a web map would. We do not operate those servers and we do not receive a copy of the requests.

Opening turn-by-turn sends the destination to Google Maps, which is a separate app with its own policy.

Practice channel

If you turn on sample drivers in Settings, the app draws fake pins and lines on your phone so you can learn the screen. They are not other people and they are not transmitted.

Radio relay you choose

Version 1.0 does not phone home to us. If you type a relay address in Settings, the app opens a connection to that server. Your handle, vehicle, car style, text, short voice clips, friend requests, and any hazard pin you publish (including a traffic-stop or camera report, with the location of that pin) are sent there so other people on that relay can hear them. Continuous GPS is not streamed. If you never set a relay, none of that leaves the phone.

Published cameras

The map includes fixed and red-light camera coordinates published by Transport for NSW. That list is stored in the app. It is not a radar detector and it does not know where a camera is operating today. Other states are not in that file. A camera, traffic-stop, or driver pin you or another driver drops is a report, not an official location. A driver report is a place and a behaviour. The app refuses notes that look like a number plate or a name request. It is not a report to the police.

What we do not do

No ads. No advertising ID. No sale of personal information. No account. No in-app purchase in version 1.0. No background location.

Children

The app is a driving tool. It is not directed at children.
