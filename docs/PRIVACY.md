# Privacy policy — 10/4 Rubberducky

The Play Console URL is the HTML copy:

https://avinconez666-cmd.github.io/rubberducky-app/privacy.html

Version 1.0 (22 October 2026).

The app runs on the phone. It does not create an account and does not upload location, voice clips, messages, or hazard pins.

- **Location (while the app is open):** map pin, Safety Mode speed, distance, hazard pin. Not collected in the background. Not shared with other drivers in this version.
- **Microphone:** push-to-talk clips stored on the phone for playback. Not uploaded.
- **Local storage:** handle, vehicle, channels, convoy names, hazard notes, message log. Deleted when the app is uninstalled.
- **OpenStreetMap tiles and Nominatim search:** the device requests tiles and place names directly. We do not receive a copy.
- **Google Maps:** only if you tap “Open turn-by-turn”. That is Google’s app.
- **Practice channel:** fake pins and lines drawn on the phone. Not people. Not transmitted.
- No ads, no advertising ID, no sale of data, no in-app purchase in 1.0.
